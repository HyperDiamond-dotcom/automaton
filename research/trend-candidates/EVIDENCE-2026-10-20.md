# Strategy comparison: Heikin-Ashi vs Donchian vs momentum — 20 October 2026

**Result: no strategy meets a real-money deployment gate.** The 252-trading-day momentum filter is a *research candidate*, not a validated source of excess returns. These tests are an approximation of published ideas, not exact replications of any academic portfolio.

## Test implementation

Files: `etf-study.mjs` and `etf-study.test.mjs` in this directory. Five predeclared US-listed ETFs: QQQ (Nasdaq 100), SPY (S&P 500), IWM (Russell 2000), GLD (gold), TLT (long US Treasury bonds).

- Yahoo Finance chart **daily original OHLC**, dates **2 January 2015 to 19 October 2026**, 2,966 bars per ETF. A later re-fetch may change response metadata and SHA256.
- All entries/exits use genuine recorded market OHLC prices, **not** synthetic Heikin-Ashi candle values.
- Fictional **$5,000 cash** per ETF and period, no leverage or shorts, trade size min(35% account notional, $25 initial risk at a 2×14-day ATR stop). Fractional US ETF shares are assumed for simulation.
- Signals execute at the **following session open**, at an adverse slippage of 5 basis points each side and $0.50 fee each side. Stop orders use the daily low and adverse opening gap. 5% observed portfolio drawdown disables subsequent entries, not a guaranteed ceiling.
- **Heikin-Ashi**: 2 green HA candles plus close above SMA50; exit two red HA or below SMA50.
- **Donchian**: closing breakout above previous 20-day high; exit below prior 10-day low.
- **Momentum**: closing positive 252-day return; evaluate at month end, enter/exit next open. This differs from the original diversified time-series-momentum literature.
- **Baseline**: buy-and-hold with up to 35% allocation, same simulated trading costs, 65% cash, no account interest. Active strategies often use less capital so the benchmark is **not equal-risk**.

## Mean of five independent simulations, as percent of initial $5,000 each

| Strategy | 2017–2022 development | 2023–2025 later historical sample | Jan–19 Oct 2026 later historical sample |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Heikin-Ashi + SMA | +2.189% | +1.649% | +0.470% |
| Donchian 20/10 | +2.871% | +0.948% | +0.232% |
| 252-day momentum | +2.995% | +2.637% | +0.377% |
| 35% passive buy-and-hold | +18.106% | +25.293% | +1.528% |

These percentages are NOT annualised returns and NOT the performance of a constructed combined portfolio; each ETF-period is an isolated simulation resetting to $5k, and the number is the arithmetic mean across five tickers.

## Typical mean *maximum mark-to-market* drawdown, by the same five isolated runs

| Strategy | 2017–2022 | 2023–2025 | 2026 to 19 Oct |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Heikin-Ashi | 3.640% | 2.774% | 1.313% |
| Donchian | 2.837% | 2.855% | 1.258% |
| Momentum | 2.988% | 2.344% | 1.190% |
| 35%-invested passive | 17.265% | 9.369% | 5.630% |

**Material trade-off:** apparent drawdown savings reflect lower average market exposure and cash drag; the strategy may not have a superior *risk-adjusted* edge after dividends and opportunity costs.

## NQ futures cross-check

The separate `study.mjs` source in this folder also explored 2024–2026 **unadjusted/rolled NQ futures** 1-minute source. Its one-MNQ-contract 2×daily ATR risk vastly exceeded the intended 0.5% per-trade risk and triggered early drawdown halts. **Do not treat that exploratory NQ replay as decision-grade evidence or as a reason to trade futures.** The five-ETF model above uses risk-sized fractional share positions instead.

## Decision

1. Keep Heikin-Ashi as a *secondary visual filter* rather than Atlas's main execution system. Tradable prices are never HA prices.
2. Keep the monthly 252-day long-only momentum rule as a possible risk-reduction overlay for **paper evaluation only**. Do not claim it beats passive investing; on this sample it did not.
3. Do not switch on live stock, crypto or futures trading; no Railway deployment and no funded-account purchases.
4. Any future experiment needs fresh, unseen data, a portfolio-level allocation/fee/tax model, a more realistic matched-risk benchmark, and forward-only paper observation. Be especially careful that these are US-listed tickers, not necessarily accessible to UK retail accounts; validate eligible UCITS equivalents before any trading design.
